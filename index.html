About Us

Recovering the 417 is a locally operated drone services company serving Missouri and surrounding areas, based out of Buffalo, MO. Our FAA-certified pilots bring years of experience in aerial operations, thermal imaging, and emergency response support.

We are committed to fast response times, professional results, and working closely with clients to meet their specific needs — whether it's a missing person search or a routine property inspection.
